# Resort Owner Questionnaire

What to ask a resort owner before writing the documents in their knowledge
base, how their guests actually ask the same things (for testing the chat),
and a checklist for the first meeting.

The chat answers from the documents and refuses what they do not say, so
every answer here is worth writing down, even "it depends, message us".

## Before you start

Two rules that shape every answer:

1. **Facts stated as rules are what the chat needs.** "Check-in is 2 PM"
   works. So does "we decide case by case, message us", as long as it is
   written down.
2. **Rates and availability do not go in the documents.** Room and package
   prices live on the **Rooms** screen, holiday dates and surcharges on the
   **Holidays** screen, and the chat quotes them from the database. The
   documents hold the *rules* around prices (what a rate includes, who counts
   as a guest), never the figures.

Each section below names the sample document its answers feed
(`01`–`08` in this folder). Sections marked **essential** are needed before
launch; **recommended** ones can follow once the resort's own guests show
what they ask.

## Part A: Questions for the owner

### 1. The business and how to reach it (essential, → `01-resort-overview`)

- What is the resort's full name, and the short name guests use?
- Where exactly is it: address, barangay, town, and the landmarks people know?
- What kind of place is it: private resort, inn, rooms, cottages? How many of each?
- Is it private (one group at a time) or shared with other guests?
- Is it open every day? What are the operating hours?
- Who runs it, and how should the assistant refer to them?
- What should the assistant be called, and what personality should it have: formal, warm, playful?
- Which contact number and app (Viber, SMS, Messenger) may the chat give out, and at what hours?
- Do you have a Google Maps or Waze pin? Is it accurate?
- What is the maximum number of guests the property allows, and why (permit, septic, parking)?

### 2. Rooms, cottages and capacity (essential, → `01` and `03`; the figures → Rooms screen)

- What accommodations do you offer: room types, cottages, the whole property?
- For each: how many beds, air-conditioning, own bathroom, view?
- How many guests does each hold as standard, and at most?
- Can guests add people above the standard? What is the rule (per head, cash on arrival)?
- Are children counted? From what age?
- Are extra beds or mattresses available?
- What is provided in a room: pillows, blankets, towels, toiletries?
- Which rooms or areas should the booking page show photos of?

### 3. Packages, inclusions and pricing rules (essential, → `02-rates-and-schedules`, rules only)

- What are the booking options: entrance only, day tour, overnight, 22-hour or whole day, per room, per night?
- What does each include: rooms, pool, kitchen, LPG, water, electricity, Wi-Fi, cleaning?
- What does each *not* include: food, ice, charcoal, towels, cook, lifeguard?
- Which days count as "weekend" for pricing?
- Which holidays get holiday rates? Which dates carry a surcharge, and which are never offered?
- Is there an electricity cap or a meter reading?
- Do children, senior citizens or PWDs get a different rate?
- Are there extra fees for the videoke, the grill or other facilities?
- Do you offer group packages, special rates or discounts?
- Is there a security deposit? How much, when is it returned, what is deducted?

### 4. Booking and reservations (essential, → `04-booking-and-payment`)

- How does a guest book today, and what should the chat say now that the booking page exists?
- What information does a guest give to book?
- What confirms a booking: a request, a reservation fee, full payment?
- How long is a date held without payment, if at all?
- How does a guest know the booking is confirmed? Do they get a reference?
- Can guests book the same day? Walk in without a reservation?
- How many rooms or cottages can one booking hold? Can the whole resort be reserved exclusively?
- Can a large group book, and is there a limit?

### 5. Payment and proof of payment (essential, → `04-booking-and-payment`)

- How much is the reservation fee per option? Is it refundable? Deducted from the total?
- Which payment methods do you take: GCash, bank transfer, cash? Cards? Cheques?
- What are the account name and number for each? (Only the owner's verified details go in the document.)
- Where does a guest send proof of payment, and should they include their reference?
- How long does verification take, and how does the guest hear back?
- When and how is the balance paid: before arrival or at the resort?
- What should a guest do if they sent the wrong amount, or the payment has not been confirmed?

### 6. Check-in, check-out and the stay (essential, → `02` and `08`)

- What time is check-in and check-out for each option? Is checkout firm?
- What time does each package start and end, and how many hours does it cover?
- Can guests check in early or check out late? At what fee?
- Can guests arrive before check-in and wait inside? Where?
- What happens if guests arrive late?
- Can guests extend? At what cost, and when is it refused?
- Is night swimming allowed? Until when?

### 7. House rules (essential, → `05-house-rules`)

- Quiet hours? Music, karaoke and speakers: allowed, until when, how loud?
- What happens if more people arrive at the gate than were declared?
- Outside food and drinks: allowed? Is there corkage, and exactly when does it apply?
- Alcohol: allowed? Any limits? Glass near the pool?
- Smoking: where allowed, where not?
- Pets: allowed, which sizes, how many, at what fee, where can they not go?
- Pool rules: hours, diving, floats, children without an adult, lifeguard?
- Visitors who are not staying: allowed, counted, charged?
- Caterers, vendors, DJs, photographers: allowed? Any fee or rule?
- Guests' own cooking equipment, charcoal, fireworks, bonfires, drones: allowed? Where?
- Decorations: allowed? Restrictions (nails, tape, confetti, balloons)?
- Cleaning before leaving? Trash?
- Damage and breakage: how are they charged? Penalties for breaking rules?
- What has caused an argument with a guest before? (Those are the rules worth writing down.)

### 8. Cancellation, rescheduling and refunds (essential, → `04-booking-and-payment`)

- Can a guest cancel? Is the reservation fee refundable?
- Can a booking be rescheduled? How many times, how many days ahead, free or with a fee?
- What happens on a same-day cancellation or a no-show?
- Can a reservation be transferred to another person?
- What happens in bad weather, or if the resort closes for a typhoon or a power outage?
- How long does an approved refund take?

### 9. Amenities and facilities (recommended, → `03-amenities-and-facilities`)

- Pools: how many, sizes, depths, adult and kiddie, hot spring or heated, jacuzzi, hours?
- Kitchen: stove, LPG, refrigerator, rice cooker, microwave, cookware, plates, utensils?
- Grill and grilling area? Charcoal provided, or bought where?
- Function hall or event area: size, tables, chairs, sound system, projector?
- Videoke? Life vests? Anything for rent?
- Wi-Fi: available, coverage, and may the chat give the password?
- Drinking water provided? Refill nearby?
- Shower rooms and restrooms: how many?
- Generator or backup power? Water pressure problems?
- Nearest sari-sari store, market, pharmacy, hospital, ATM?
- Are all amenities included in the price, or are some charged?

### 10. Food, drinks and cooking (recommended, → `03` and `05`)

- Is there a restaurant, food packages or catering?
- Can guests bring their own food and cook? What is provided for cooking?
- Do you sell ice, drinking water, soft drinks, charcoal?
- Can meals be requested in advance?

### 11. Getting there, parking and accessibility (recommended, → `06-directions-and-getting-there`)

- Directions from the nearest city or highway, with landmarks?
- Public transport: which bus, jeep, tricycle, the usual fare and time?
- What do drivers usually get wrong? Is the gate hard to find? Is there a sign? Who opens it?
- Parking: how many vehicles, inside or on the street, free? Can vans, SUVs and buses enter?
- Is the road passable for low-clearance vehicles?
- Do you offer a shuttle or pickup?
- Steps, ramps, steep paths: is it friendly to seniors and PWDs?

### 12. Events and large groups (recommended, → `07-events-and-add-ons`)

- Which events are allowed: birthdays, reunions, company outings, weddings?
- What is the maximum number of guests for an event?
- Which add-ons can be arranged, at what fee, and how far in advance: cook, extra mattresses, floats, projector, tents, lechon?
- Do you provide tables and chairs for events? Is there an event fee or an event package?
- What is *not* offered that guests often ask for?

### 13. Safety, weather and emergencies (recommended, → `08-faq`)

- Lifeguard on duty? First-aid kit? A designated children's area?
- Security at night: CCTV, a caretaker on site?
- What changes when it rains: pool, power, rates? Is swimming allowed in bad weather?
- What happens in a power interruption?
- Who does a guest contact in an emergency?

### 14. Changes and support (recommended, → `08-faq` and the chat settings)

- Can a guest change the guest count, the room or the package after booking? How?
- Can the name on a reservation be changed? What if a guest loses their reference?
- How does a guest reach a person, and when should the chat hand over?
- What should the chat say to a special request?

### 15. The chat's own settings (not documents: tenant settings and screens)

- The fallback message when the chat cannot answer, in English, Taglish and
  Tagalog, with the number to contact (`tenant:fallback`).
- Which languages guests use, and whether replies should default to Taglish.
- The booking page's "what happens next" text, shown after a request is sent.
- The booking page's guest hint (for example "do not count children aged 3 and below").
- The holiday calendar for this year and next, with each surcharge (Holidays screen).
- Room names, capacities and rates (Rooms screen), never in a document.

### 16. Owner's homework before writing

- Anything that changed in the last year that old brochures or posts still show.
- Anything the chat should *not* answer and refer to a person instead: disputes, refunds, discounts.
- 20 to 50 real questions from their Messenger or Viber conversations, with
  names, numbers and payment details removed. These show what guests really
  ask, including what the owner forgot to mention, and they become the test
  questions before launch.

## Part C: Where each answer comes from

| Question type | Answered from | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rules and facts | The documents (knowledge base) | check-in times, inclusions, house rules, cancellation policy, directions |
| Prices, availability, a booking | The database, through the chat's tool and the booking page | "is 15 October free?", "how much is the Overnight on Saturday?", sending a request |
| Anything else | A person, via the fallback message | payment disputes, refunds, discounts, complaints, special requests |

The documents alone never confirm a date or quote a figure: the chat checks
the database for both.

## Part D: The first meeting, in fifteen answers

Do not send the owner every question at once. These fifteen are enough to
launch; the rest follows from what their guests then ask.

1. **Basics**
   - [ ] Resort name, address, map pin, contact number and app
   - [ ] Operating hours and directions
   - [ ] Amenities and facilities
2. **Accommodation** (figures → Rooms screen)
   - [ ] Room types, standard and maximum capacity
   - [ ] Extra-guest rule and child rule
   - [ ] What each package includes
3. **Booking and payment**
   - [ ] How to book and what a guest provides
   - [ ] Reservation fee, payment methods, verified account details
   - [ ] Proof of payment and how verification works
4. **Policies**
   - [ ] Check-in, check-out and the stay
   - [ ] Cancellation, rescheduling and refunds
   - [ ] House rules: outside food, pets, videoke
5. **Support**
   - [ ] The questions guests ask most, from real conversations
   - [ ] How and when a person takes over (the fallback message)
   - [ ] Common complaints and the approved answers
