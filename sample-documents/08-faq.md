# Villa Amihan — Frequently Asked Questions

**Is the pool really hot, or is it heated?**
It is naturally hot. The water comes from a private deep well into the Mount
Makiling geothermal aquifer at 38°C to 40°C. Nothing heats it, so there is no
"heated pool" surcharge and no waiting for it to warm up.

**Can we share the resort with another group to split the cost?**
No. Villa Amihan is exclusive-use and sells only one booking per slot per day. The
whole property goes to one group.

**Can we come in early or stay a little past checkout?**
Usually not. The pools are fully drained and refilled between bookings and the
refill takes about four hours, so the gap between slots is working time, not idle
time. An extension is ₱1,000 per hour when the next slot happens to be free.

**Do you provide towels?**
No. Guests bring their own towels and toiletries. Bedsheets, pillows and blankets
for the four bedrooms are provided.

**Is there a corkage fee?**
Not for food and drinks the guests bring themselves, including alcohol. ₱1,500
applies only when an outside caterer sets up and serves on the property.

**How many people can we bring?**
The rate covers 30. Beyond 30 it is ₱250 per head, paid in cash at the gate. The
hard maximum is 45, including children and drivers, because of the septic system's
rated load.

**Are children counted?**
Children aged three and below are free and are not counted toward the 30. They
still count toward the 45-person hard cap.

**Can we bring our dog?**
Yes, small and medium pets at ₱500 each, maximum three. Pets are not allowed in
the pools, on the beds, or inside the bedrooms.

**Until what time can we use the videoke?**
10:00 PM, under Barangay Ordinance 2018-04. Swimming and conversation can continue
after that; amplified sound cannot.

**Is there a lifeguard?**
No, and one cannot be hired as an add-on. Adults in the group supervise their own
children.

**Can we pay the balance by GCash or bank transfer?**
No. Only the reservation fee is paid electronically. The balance and the ₱2,000
security deposit are paid in cash on arrival.

**Is the reservation fee refundable?**
No, at any notice period. A booking can be rescheduled once for free with at least
seven days' notice, to a date within 60 days.

**Can we book 31 December?**
Yes, from 1 September of that year, first-come first-served on payment of the
reservation fee. That date carries a ₱4,000 peak surcharge on top of the holiday
rate and cannot be rescheduled.

**Can a bus or coaster get to the resort?**
No. The last 80 metres of Sampaguita Lane is one vehicle wide. Coasters drop the
group at the mouth of the lane.

**Is there Wi-Fi?**
Yes, a 50 Mbps fibre line reaching the house and the pool deck. The network is
VillaAmihan_5G.

**Can we hold a wedding there?**
No. The function area is too small for a ceremony setup and the barangay permit
covers residential use only. Small wedding after-parties are fine.

**Do you sell charcoal and ice?**
No. The sari-sari store at the corner of Sampaguita Lane sells charcoal at around
₱80 a bag and ice in blocks.
