# Villa Amihan — Events and Add-Ons

## What the resort is used for

Villa Amihan is most often booked for family reunions, children's birthday
parties, company team outings, debut celebrations and small wedding after-parties.
Roughly two-thirds of bookings are family groups.

Villa Amihan does not host weddings or wedding ceremonies. The function area seats
24 at table and cannot take a ceremony setup, and the barangay permit covers
residential use only.

## Add-ons

A cook can be arranged for ₱1,500 per day. The cook prepares food the guests bring
and cleans the kitchen afterwards, but does not shop for ingredients or serve at
table.

An extra set of eight foam mattresses and pillows can be rented for ₱800, bringing
the floor sleeping capacity from 8 to 16. This is worth arranging in advance, as
the extra set is borrowed from the owners' own house.

A 10-foot inflatable pool float can be used for ₱500, which covers the cost of
re-inflating and drying it. It is only allowed in the main pool.

Balloon and backdrop setup by an outside supplier is allowed at no charge,
provided nothing is nailed, taped or stapled to the painted walls. Command hooks
and freestanding frames are fine.

A projector and a 100-inch pull-down screen can be set up in the function area for
₱1,000. The projector is HDMI only; guests bring their own adapter.

## What is not available

There is no catering service, no in-house food, and no arrangement with any
caterer. Groups arrange their own.

There is no lifeguard service available, even as a paid add-on. The owners looked
into it in 2022 and could not get an insurer to cover it at a sensible price.

There is no airport or hotel transfer service. Groups arrange their own transport.

There is no event coordinator, sound system rental, or lights-and-sound package.
The videoke machine is the only audio equipment on the property.
