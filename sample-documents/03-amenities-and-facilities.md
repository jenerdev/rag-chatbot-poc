# Villa Amihan — Amenities and Facilities

## Pools

The main pool measures 25 feet by 15 feet. The shallow end is 3.5 feet deep and
the deep end is 4.5 feet deep, so it is a swimming pool with no diving area. The
depth is painted in white tiles on the pool wall at both ends.

The kiddie pool measures 10 feet by 8 feet and is a uniform 1.5 feet deep. It is
fed from the same hot spring line as the main pool but sits under the acacia
tree, so it usually runs two to three degrees cooler than the main pool.

Both pools are completely drained, scrubbed and refilled between every booking.
A full refill takes about four hours, which is why there is a gap between
checkout and the next check-in and why early arrival cannot be accommodated.

There is no lifeguard on duty at Villa Amihan at any time. Adults in the group
are responsible for supervising children in and around both pools.

## Bedrooms

There are four air-conditioned bedrooms, named after the owners' grandmothers:
Dalisay, Marikit, Liwayway and Tala.

Dalisay is the largest bedroom. It has one queen bed and one double-deck bunk,
sleeping five, and is the only bedroom with its own toilet and bath.

Marikit has one queen bed and sleeps two. Liwayway has two double-deck bunks and
sleeps four. Tala has one double bed and one single bed and sleeps three.

The four bedrooms sleep 14 people in actual beds. The remaining guests sleep on
the floor of the function area, and Villa Amihan provides eight foam mattresses
and sixteen pillows for this. Bedsheets and blankets are provided for the beds
but not for the floor mattresses.

## Bathrooms

There are three toilet-and-bath units in total: one inside Dalisay, one in the
hallway serving the other three bedrooms, and one outdoor shower room beside the
pool deck. The outdoor shower has cold water only.

## Kitchen and dining

The kitchen has a four-burner gas stove with LPG included, a two-door
refrigerator, a microwave, an electric kettle, a rice cooker that holds 10 cups
of uncooked rice, and a water dispenser that takes standard five-gallon bottles.

Plates, glasses, cutlery, serving bowls, a chopping board and knives are provided
for 30 people. Guests wash their own dishes; the caretakers do not do this.

The function area has two long dining tables that seat 12 each, plus 20 monobloc
chairs that can be moved to the pool deck.

## Grilling area

The grilling area at the side of the house has two charcoal grills and a concrete
prep counter with a sink. Charcoal is not included. The sari-sari store at the
corner of Sampaguita Lane sells charcoal at around ₱80 per bag.

## Other facilities

Wi-Fi is available throughout the house and reaches the pool deck. The network
name is VillaAmihan_5G and the password is amihanpansol2019. The connection is a
50 Mbps fibre line, which is fine for streaming but not for 30 people at once.

There is one videoke machine in the function area, with the song book kept in the
drawer of the console. Its amplifier is set to a fixed maximum volume the guests
cannot raise, because of the barangay noise ordinance.

There is a 43-inch smart TV in the function area with Netflix and YouTube. Guests
sign in to their own accounts and are asked to sign out before leaving.

Parking fits six cars inside the gate. Additional vehicles park along Sampaguita
Lane on the left side only, as the right side is a fire lane and the barangay
tows vehicles parked there.
