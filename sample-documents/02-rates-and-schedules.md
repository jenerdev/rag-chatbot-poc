# Villa Amihan — Rates and Booking Schedules

Villa Amihan offers three booking slots: Day Tour, Overnight, and the 22-Hour
Whole Day. Only one slot is sold per day, so a Day Tour booking and an Overnight
booking never overlap on the same date.

The Day Tour slot runs from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. It has a weekday rate (Monday
to Thursday) and a higher weekend-and-holiday rate (Friday to Sunday, and any
declared regular or special non-working holiday). The exact rate for a date is
shown by the booking page and by this chat's availability check.

The Overnight slot runs from 2:00 PM to 12:00 noon the following day. It has a
weekday rate and a higher weekend-and-holiday rate. Night swimming is included,
but the pool lights are switched off at 12:00 midnight.

The 22-Hour Whole Day slot runs from 8:00 AM to 6:00 AM the following day. It is
the dearest of the three, with a weekday rate and a higher weekend-and-holiday
rate, and the only one that covers both a full daytime swim and a full night
swim.

All three rates cover a standard number of guests. Each guest above that number
is charged a per-head fee, payable in cash on arrival; the number included and
the fee are shown with every price. Children aged three and below are not counted
and are not charged.

Holiday rates also apply on the four days of Holy Week from Maundy Thursday to
Easter Sunday, on 24, 25 and 31 December, on 1 January, and on the Calamba city
fiesta on 12 February — even when those dates fall on a Monday to Thursday.

December 24, December 25 and December 31 carry an additional peak surcharge on
top of the holiday rate, shown with the price for those dates. These three dates
are usually fully booked by September and are not offered for rescheduling.

The booking page (the Book button at the top of this chat) shows the exact price
for any date, with the weekend, holiday and extra-guest charges already added.
This chat can also give the price for a date: say the date, and the number of
guests if it is above the standard.

The rate includes the exclusive use of the whole property, the hot spring pools,
all four air-conditioned bedrooms, the function area, the kitchen, the grilling
area, LPG for the stove, water, electricity, Wi-Fi, and the turnover cleaning
after checkout.

The rate does not include food, drinking water, charcoal, ice, a cook, a
lifeguard, or towels. Guests bring their own towels and toiletries; Villa Amihan
does not provide them at any rate tier.

Electricity is included in the rate with no cap and no meter reading on arrival
or departure. This is because the four air-conditioning units are inverter models
and the owners decided a metered charge was not worth the arguments it caused.

Extending past the checkout time is charged at ₱1,000 per hour or any part of an
hour, and is only possible when there is no booking in the next slot. Because the
pool must be drained and refilled between bookings, an extension is usually
refused rather than priced.
