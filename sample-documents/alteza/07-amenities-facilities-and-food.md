# Alteza Hillside Resort — Amenities, Facilities, Food and Cooking

## Pools

There are two pools on the lower level. The main pool is 12 metres long,
4 feet deep at the shallow end and 6 feet at the deep end, unheated and
fed from the resort's own spring. The kiddie pool beside it is 2 feet deep
throughout. Both are cleaned every Wednesday morning. There is no jacuzzi
and no hot pool. Shower rooms and restrooms are beside the pool deck, two
for women and two for men, in addition to the bathrooms in the rooms.
Life vests for children are available at reception free of charge, with a
deposit that is returned with the vest.

## Kitchen and cooking

The shared kitchen beside the function hall has two induction stoves, a
large refrigerator with a shelf per room, a microwave, a rice cooker, an
electric kettle, cooking pots and pans, plates, glasses and utensils.
Guests bring their own food and cook for themselves. The grilling area at
the edge of the garden has two charcoal grills, free to use; charcoal is
sold at reception by the bag, and guests may bring their own. Guests may
also bring their own portable stove or grill for the grilling area.

The resort has no restaurant and no food packages. A cook can be arranged
for a fee (see the events and add-ons document), and the family can
recommend a lechon supplier and a caterer in Tanay town, who deal with the
guests directly. Reception sells ice by the bag, bottled soft drinks and
mineral water, and charcoal; it does not sell alcohol. Drinking water is
free from the refill station beside the kitchen.

## Function hall

The covered function hall seats 60 at round tables. Tables and chairs for
up to 60 are included. A sound system with two speakers and a microphone,
a projector with a screen (HDMI only; bring your own adapter), and a
videoke machine are rented from reception. The hall is shared by all
guests unless an event has reserved it, in which case other guests are
told at booking.

## Wi-Fi, power and water

Wi-Fi covers the rooms, the reception and the function hall, and is weak
at the kubos and the pool deck. The password is given at check-in and is
not given out by this chat. The resort has a diesel generator that starts
automatically in a power interruption and runs the lights, the
refrigerator, the water pump and the Wi-Fi, but not the air-conditioning.
Water comes from the resort's own spring and pressure is steady; the
showers are hot in every room.

## Around the resort

There is a sari-sari store 300 metres down the road, at the chapel, which
sells snacks, drinks, ice and basic goods and closes at 9:00 PM. The
nearest market, pharmacy, ATM and gas station are in Tanay town proper,
about twenty minutes away by car. The nearest hospital is Tanay General
Hospital, also in the town proper.

## Accessibility

The resort is on a slope. The parking area, the reception, Casa Uno and
the Loft Suite's stair are on the top level; Casa Dos and the function
hall are one flight of steps down; Familia, the kubos and the pools are
another flight down. There are handrails but no ramps, so wheelchairs
cannot reach the pool level. Casa Uno is the room recommended for senior
guests.
