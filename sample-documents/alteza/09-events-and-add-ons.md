# Alteza Hillside Resort — Events and Add-Ons

## Events

Alteza hosts birthdays, family reunions, company outings and team
buildings, and small weddings and christenings. An event uses the function
hall, which seats 60 at round tables, and the garden. The most guests an
event can bring onto the property is 40 on a normal booking, because that
is the property's limit, or 60 when the event has exclusive use of the
whole resort, which is arranged directly with the owners and is not booked
on the page. An event of more than 60 guests cannot be held.

An event that reserves the function hall for itself pays an event fee of
₱3,000 for the day, in addition to the rooms it books; the other guests
are told at booking that the hall is reserved. An event that does not
reserve the hall pays no fee and shares it. Decorations may be tied or
taped with painter's tape only: no nails, tacks, glue, confetti or glitter.
Balloons are allowed. Guests may bring their own caterer, who pays the
₱2,000 corkage described in the house rules, or their own food with no
fee. There are no event packages; an event is the rooms, the hall fee and
any add-ons below.

## Add-ons

These are arranged at booking or at reception and paid in cash on the day.

A cook can be arranged for ₱1,500 per day. The cook prepares the food the
guests bring, from breakfast to dinner, and cleans the kitchen after; the
cook does not buy the food. Book the cook at least three days ahead.

An extra set of six foam mattresses with pillows and blankets can be rented
for ₱600 per night, for the Familia room only, bringing its floor sleeping
capacity to six comfortably.

The videoke machine is rented for ₱1,000 per night, used in the function
hall until 10:00 PM.

The sound system with two speakers and a microphone is ₱1,500 per day. The
projector and screen are ₱800 per day; the projector is HDMI only, so
guests bring their own adapter.

A canopy tent, 3 by 6 metres, can be set up in the garden for ₱2,500 per
day; book it at least three days ahead. Extra tables and chairs beyond the
sixty in the hall are not available.

A lechon can be ordered through the family's supplier in Tanay town, who
delivers to the gate on the day and is paid directly; the family passes
on the current price when asked. A caterer in Tanay can be recommended the
same way.

## What Alteza does not offer

There is no event coordinator, no lights-and-sound package, no DJ, no
photographer, no massage service, no boat or hiking tour, and no shuttle.
The family can recommend a hiking guide for the Daraitan trails, who deals
with guests directly.
