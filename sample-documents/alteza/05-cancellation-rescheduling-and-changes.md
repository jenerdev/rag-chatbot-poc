# Alteza Hillside Resort — Cancellation, Rescheduling and Changes

## Rescheduling

A confirmed booking may be rescheduled once, free of charge, if the request
is made at least seven days before the booked date. The new date must be
within 90 days of the original and must have the same rooms open. If the
new date falls on a weekend or holiday rate where the original was a
weekday, the difference is added to the balance; if it falls on a lower
rate, the difference is not refunded.

A reschedule requested less than seven days before the booked date forfeits
the reservation fee. A new booking then needs a new reservation fee.

## Cancellation

The reservation fee is non-refundable. A booking cancelled at any time
loses the fee and nothing more; no balance is owed for a cancelled booking.
A no-show, or a group that arrives on a different date without having
rescheduled, is treated as a cancellation.

A booking may be transferred to another person: message the reference and
the new guest's name and mobile number at least a day before the date. The
new guest pays the balance on arrival.

## Weather and closures

If a typhoon signal is raised over Tanay for the booked date, or the road
from Tanay town is closed, the resort closes and every booking for that
date is rescheduled free of charge to any date within 90 days, without the
seven-day rule. The family messages every booked guest the day before when
a closure is likely. Rain alone does not close the resort: the pools stay
open in ordinary rain and close only during thunder and lightning, and the
rooms and the covered function hall are usable. No refund is given for
rain.

If the resort has to cancel a confirmed booking for any other reason, the
reservation fee is refunded in full by GCash within three working days.

## Changing a booking

Before arrival, the number of guests, the rooms and the period can be
changed by messaging the reference, as long as the rooms asked for are
open. A change that raises the total is paid on arrival; a change that
lowers it reduces the balance, and the reservation fee is never refunded
even if the new total is lower than the fee already paid. The name on a
booking is changed the same way. A guest who has lost the booking
reference can recover it by messaging the mobile number and email used
when booking.
