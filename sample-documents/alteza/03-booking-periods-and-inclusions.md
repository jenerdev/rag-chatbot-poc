# Alteza Hillside Resort — Booking Periods, Inclusions and Pricing Rules

Alteza sells two booking periods, both by the room. There is no entrance
fee and no walk-in pool use: only guests with a room booking use the
resort.

The Day Use period runs from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM on one date. It is meant
for a family that wants the pool and a room to rest and change in for the
day.

The Overnight period runs from 2:00 PM to 12:00 noon the following day.
A stay of several nights is several Overnight periods in a row on the same
room: check-in at 2:00 PM on the first day, check-out at 12:00 noon on the
last. A two-night stay, for example, is three days and two nights.

Each period has a weekday rate and a higher weekend rate for every room.
Weekend rates apply on Fridays, Saturdays and Sundays, on regular and
special non-working holidays, and on the four days of Holy Week from
Maundy Thursday to Easter Sunday. 24, 25 and 31 December and 1 January are
peak dates: 24 and 31 December and 1 January carry a peak surcharge on top
of the weekend rate, and the resort is closed on 25 December. The exact
rate for any date, with the weekend or holiday rate and the surcharge
already applied, is shown by the booking page and by this chat's
availability check.

A room's rate covers a standard number of guests, stated with every price.
Each guest above that number, up to the room's maximum, pays the per-head
extra-guest fee for the period. Children aged five and below are not
counted.

The rate includes the room, the use of the main pool and the kiddie pool
during pool hours, the garden, the shared kitchen, the grilling area, the
function hall when no event has reserved it, parking, Wi-Fi, drinking
water from the refill station, electricity with no cap or meter reading,
and cleaning of the room after checkout.

The rate does not include food, ice, charcoal, cooked meals, a cook, the
videoke, the sound system or projector, event decorations, or a lifeguard.
Those that can be arranged are described in the events and add-ons
document, with their fees.

Senior citizens and persons with disability get the discount required by
law on the room rate on presentation of a valid ID at check-in, applied to
the balance paid on arrival; the booking page shows the undiscounted rate.
There are no other discounts, and no group packages: a group books the
rooms it needs at the room rates.

Extending past checkout is possible only when the room has no booking in
the next period, and is charged per hour or any part of an hour at the fee
stated at reception. Extending past 5:00 PM on a Day Use into the night is
not an extension; it is an Overnight booking and is charged as one.
