# Alteza Hillside Resort — Booking, Payment and Proof of Payment

## How to book

Bookings are made on Alteza's booking page. To open it, press the Book
button at the top of this chat. Choose the period, the date, the number of
nights for an Overnight stay, the rooms and the number of guests in each,
and the page shows the exact price. Then enter your name, mobile number
and email and send the request.

A booking request is not yet a booking. The family replies, usually within
the same day and always within 24 hours, on the mobile number given, to
confirm that the rooms are open and to ask for the reservation fee. The
reply carries your booking reference, a code such as ALT-7KQ4MX, which you
quote in every message about the booking.

Questions can also go to 0918-224-7710 on Viber or SMS between 8:00 AM and
9:00 PM. This chat can check which rooms are open on a date and quote the
price, but it cannot make, change or confirm a booking; only the booking
page and the family can.

Same-day bookings are accepted when a room is open, but the reservation
fee must be received before arrival. Walk-ins without a booking are
admitted only if a room is open at that moment and the fee is paid at the
gate. One request may hold up to six rooms; a group larger than the six
rooms cannot be accommodated. Exclusive use of the whole resort is arranged
directly with the owners and is not booked on the page.

## Reservation fee and balance

A room is held only once the reservation fee is received. Alteza does not
hold rooms on a message or a promise, and a request that has not been paid
within 48 hours of the family's reply is released.

The reservation fee is half of the total shown on the booking page,
rounded up to the nearest hundred pesos. It is non-refundable but is
deducted from the total.

The fee is paid by GCash to 0918-224-7710 under the name "Alteza Hillside
Resort", or by bank transfer to BPI current account 4419-0032-56 under the
same name. These are the only accounts the resort uses; a request to pay
any other account is not from the resort. Cards, cheques and online
payment links are not accepted.

After paying, send a screenshot of the transfer on Viber or SMS with your
booking reference. The family checks the account and replies to confirm,
usually within a few hours and always within 24 hours. The booking is
confirmed by that reply, not by the transfer alone. If you sent the wrong
amount, say so in the same message: a shortfall is added to the balance,
and an overpayment is deducted from it. If no confirmation arrives within
24 hours, message the number again with the reference and the time of the
transfer.

The balance is paid in cash on arrival, at reception, before the group
settles in. A refundable security deposit of ₱2,000 per booking is
collected in cash together with the balance and returned at checkout after
the caretakers check the rooms, less the cost of any breakage or missing
item.
