# Alteza Hillside Resort — Overview

Alteza Hillside Resort, called simply "Alteza" by its guests, is a small
family-run resort on the foothills of the Sierra Madre in Barangay Sampaloc,
Tanay, Rizal. It is a shared resort with six rooms, not an exclusive-use
property: several families or groups can stay at the same time, each in
their own room or cottage, and they share the pools, the garden and the
function hall. Exclusive use of the whole resort can be arranged directly
with the owners for events, but it is not the normal way to book.

The property address is Km 58, Sampaloc–Daraitan Road, Barangay Sampaloc,
Tanay, Rizal. It is about twenty minutes by car from Tanay town proper,
past the Sampaloc chapel. The gate is painted green and stands about 200
metres after the chapel on the right-hand side; a small wooden sign reads
"Alteza". The Google Maps and Waze pin is "Alteza Hillside Resort Tanay"
and it is accurate to the gate.

The resort is run by the Villanueva family, who live on the property. The
owners are Mr. and Mrs. Villanueva; day-to-day, guests deal with the family
and two caretakers who are on site around the clock. The assistant on this
chat is called Tessa, and she speaks for the resort in a warm, plain way,
in English, Tagalog or Taglish, whichever the guest uses.

Alteza is open every day of the year except Christmas Day (25 December),
when the family closes it. Reception is staffed from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM;
outside those hours a caretaker answers the gate for guests who are
staying. Questions and bookings by message are answered between 8:00 AM
and 9:00 PM on Viber or SMS at 0918-224-7710. The same number is used for
GCash payments.

The resort takes at most 40 guests on the property at any one time,
counting children, drivers and visitors. The limit comes from the septic
system and the size of the parking area, not from the rooms: even when the
rooms could sleep more, the count stops at 40. Guests above the number
declared at booking are charged the per-head fee and are admitted only
while the property is under 40.

The property is suited to families and small groups. It is on a slope,
with steps between the parking area, the rooms and the pool level, so it
is not friendly to wheelchairs; guests who find stairs difficult should
ask for Casa Uno, which is on the same level as the parking area and the
reception.
