# Alteza Hillside Resort — Directions, Parking and Arrival

## By car

From Manila, take Marcos Highway east through Antipolo to Tanay town
proper, about two hours from Quezon City without traffic. In Tanay, follow
the signs for Sampaloc and Daraitan; the Sampaloc–Daraitan Road climbs for
about twenty minutes. Pass the Sampaloc chapel on the left; the resort's
green gate is about 200 metres after it, on the right, with a wooden
"Alteza" sign. The Google Maps and Waze pin "Alteza Hillside Resort Tanay"
leads to the gate. The road is paved except for the last 300 metres, which
are compacted gravel and passable by any car, including low-clearance
sedans, in dry and wet weather.

Drivers most often miss the gate by looking for a large signboard; there
is only the small wooden sign, and the gate is green, not white like the
neighbours'. The second common mistake is turning at the first chapel in
Sampaloc, which is a different, smaller chapel a kilometre before the
right one; the right chapel has a bell tower.

## By public transport

Take a bus or a UV Express to Tanay from Cubao or Shaw Boulevard; the ride
is about two and a half hours. From the Tanay public market, take a jeep
bound for Sampaloc or Daraitan and ask to be dropped at "Alteza, after the
Sampaloc chapel"; the jeep takes about thirty minutes. From the chapel, the
gate is a three-minute walk. A tricycle from Tanay town proper to the gate
takes about twenty-five minutes; agree the fare before boarding, and
tricycle drivers know the resort by name. The last jeep back to Tanay
leaves Sampaloc at around 6:00 PM. The resort does not offer a shuttle or a
pickup service.

## Parking

Parking inside the gate takes twelve cars, free of charge, on a flat
gravel area beside reception. Vans and SUVs fit; buses and coaster-sized
vehicles cannot turn on the last stretch of road and cannot enter, so a
group arriving by coaster is dropped at the chapel. Motorcycles park in
the same area. Drivers who stay count toward the 40-guest limit.

## Arrival

The gate is opened by a caretaker from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM; guests who
arrive later ring the bell at the gate and wait for the caretaker. Guests
who arrive before check-in may wait in the reception lounge and leave
their bags there, and may use the pool from 12:00 noon on the day of an
Overnight check-in if the previous group has left; the room itself is
ready at 2:00 PM. A late arrival keeps its booking as long as the family
has been messaged; a room is not given away for a late arrival on the
booked date.
