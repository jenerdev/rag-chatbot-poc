# Alteza Hillside Resort — House Rules

Alteza is shared by several groups at once, so its rules exist to keep
every group comfortable. They are explained at check-in and posted at
reception.

## Quiet hours and music

Quiet hours are 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM. Music, speakers and the videoke must
be off by 10:00 PM. During the day, speakers are allowed in the function
hall and by the pool at a volume that does not carry to the rooms; the
caretakers will ask a group to lower it, and a second request ends the
music for the day. The videoke is rented from reception and is used only
in the function hall.

## Guest count and visitors

The number of guests is declared at booking. Guests above the declared
number are admitted only while the property is under its limit of 40, and
are charged the per-head extra-guest fee in cash at reception. Visitors
who are not staying overnight count as guests while they are on the
property and are charged the same fee for a Day Use; they must leave by
10:00 PM. The caretakers keep a count at the gate.

## Food and drinks

Guests may bring their own food and drinks, including alcohol, with no
corkage fee, and may cook in the shared kitchen and at the grilling area.
A corkage fee of ₱2,000 applies only when an outside caterer sets up and
serves on the property, because a caterer uses the kitchen, the tables and
the water, and leaves more cleaning. Glass bottles and glasses are not
allowed on the pool deck; plastic cups are available at reception.
Drunk guests are not allowed in the pools.

## Pool rules

The main pool is open from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM and the kiddie pool from
7:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Night swimming is allowed until 10:00 PM, when the
pool lights go off. There is no lifeguard: children swim only with an
adult in the water or at the edge, and children below twelve are not
allowed in the main pool without an adult. Diving is not allowed; the main
pool is 4 feet at the shallow end and 6 feet at the deep end. Floats are
allowed. The pools close during thunder and lightning and for cleaning
every Wednesday from 6:00 AM to 8:00 AM.

## Smoking

Smoking is allowed only in the garden's smoking corner beside the parking
area, never in the rooms, the function hall or the pool deck. A room that
smells of smoke at checkout is charged a cleaning fee from the deposit.

## Pets

Small dogs and cats are welcome, at ₱500 per pet and at most two per
booking. Pets are not allowed in the pools, the pool deck or the function
hall, and must be on a leash or carried outside the room. Owners clean up
after them. Large dogs are not accepted.

## Property, damage and decorations

Guests are responsible for breakage and missing items in their room and in
the common areas they use; the cost is deducted from the security deposit
and any excess is paid before leaving. Decorations may be tied or taped
with painter's tape only: no nails, tacks, glue, confetti or glitter.
Fireworks and bonfires are not allowed anywhere on the property. Drones may
be flown over the garden and the valley, not over the pools or other
groups' rooms. Guests may bring their own portable stove or grill and use
it at the grilling area only.

## Checkout

Rooms are left as found, with trash in the bins provided. Checkout is
12:00 noon for Overnight and 5:00 PM for Day Use, firm, because the rooms
are cleaned for the next group. The deposit is returned after the
caretakers check the room.
