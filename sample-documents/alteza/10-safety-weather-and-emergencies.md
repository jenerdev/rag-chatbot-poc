# Alteza Hillside Resort — Safety, Weather and Emergencies

## Safety on the property

There is no lifeguard at either pool. Children swim only with an adult in
the water or at the edge, and children below twelve are not allowed in
the main pool without an adult; the kiddie pool, 2 feet deep, is the
designated children's pool. Life vests for children are free at reception.
Diving is not allowed, and the pools close during thunder and lightning.

A first-aid kit is kept at reception, and the caretakers are trained in
basic first aid. The nearest hospital is Tanay General Hospital in the town
proper, about twenty minutes away by car; in an emergency the caretakers
call the barangay ambulance, which reaches the resort in about fifteen
minutes.

Two caretakers live on the property and one is awake through the night.
CCTV covers the gate, the parking area and the reception, not the rooms
or the pool deck. The gate is locked from 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM and opened
by the caretaker on request. Guests are responsible for their own
valuables; the reception has a small safe for passports and cash on
request.

## Weather

Alteza is on the hills, so it is cooler than Manila and rain is common in
the afternoon from June to October. Ordinary rain does not close the
resort: the rooms, the covered function hall and the covered part of the
pool deck are usable, and the pools stay open. The pools close while there
is thunder or lightning and reopen thirty minutes after the last one.

When a typhoon signal is raised over Tanay for a booked date, or the road
from Tanay town is closed by the barangay, the resort closes and every
booking for that date is rescheduled free of charge, as described in the
cancellation document. The family messages every booked guest the day
before when a closure is likely, and again in the morning if the decision
changes.

## Power and water

A power interruption starts the diesel generator automatically within a
minute. The generator runs the lights, the refrigerator, the water pump,
the Wi-Fi and the reception, but not the air-conditioning or the
induction stoves; the charcoal grills work in any case. Water comes from
the resort's own spring and does not depend on the mains.

## Emergency contacts

In an emergency on the property, go to reception or call the caretakers'
number posted in every room. The family's number, 0918-224-7710, is
answered at any hour for a guest who is staying. The Tanay police and fire
stations are in the town proper; the barangay hall of Sampaloc, which
dispatches the ambulance, is beside the chapel.
