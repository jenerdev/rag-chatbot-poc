# Alteza Hillside Resort — Frequently Asked Questions

**How much is a room?**
Each room has a weekday rate and a higher weekend-and-holiday rate for the
Day Use and the Overnight period. Say the date and the number of guests,
and this chat or the booking page gives the exact price, with the
extra-guest fee and any holiday surcharge included.

**Are you available on a date?**
Ask this chat with the date and it checks which rooms are open. Sending a
request on the booking page does not hold a room; the family confirms it
and asks for the reservation fee, and the room is held once the fee is
received.

**How many people can a room take?**
Casa Uno and Casa Dos hold up to three, Familia up to six, each kubo up to
four, and the Loft Suite two. Each rate includes a standard number of
guests, shown with the price, and guests above that pay the per-head fee.
Children aged five and below are not counted.

**Can we add extra guests?**
Yes, up to the room's maximum, at the per-head extra-guest fee shown with
every price. Guests above the declared number are admitted while the
property is under 40 and pay the fee in cash at reception.

**How do we book?**
Press the Book button at the top of this chat and send a request on the
booking page. The family confirms by message and asks for the reservation
fee. This chat cannot make or confirm a booking.

**Do you accept GCash?**
Yes: GCash to 0918-224-7710 under "Alteza Hillside Resort", or a BPI
transfer to current account 4419-0032-56. Send the screenshot with your
booking reference; the booking is confirmed by reply. Cards and cheques
are not accepted.

**Is the reservation fee refundable?**
No. It is half of the total, deducted from the total, and kept on any
cancellation or no-show. One free reschedule is allowed with at least
seven days' notice.

**What time is check-in and check-out?**
Overnight is 2:00 PM to 12:00 noon the next day. Day Use is 8:00 AM to
5:00 PM. Checkout is firm because the rooms are cleaned for the next group.

**Can we check in early or check out late?**
Early arrivals can wait at reception and use the pool from noon if the
previous group has left. Late checkout and extensions are possible only
when the room has no booking in the next period, charged per hour at the
fee stated at reception.

**Can we bring our own food?**
Yes, with no corkage, and you can cook in the shared kitchen and at the
grills. The ₱2,000 corkage applies only to an outside caterer who sets up
and serves. There is no restaurant; a cook can be arranged for ₱1,500 a day.

**Are pets allowed?**
Small dogs and cats, at ₱500 per pet, at most two per booking, not in the
pools, the pool deck or the function hall. Large dogs are not accepted.

**Until what time is the videoke allowed?**
10:00 PM, in the function hall only. Quiet hours are 10:00 PM to 7:00 AM.

**Are visitors allowed?**
Yes, counted as guests while on the property and charged the per-head fee
for a Day Use; they leave by 10:00 PM.

**Do you have parking?**
Yes, free, for twelve cars, vans and SUVs. Buses and coasters cannot enter
and drop guests at the Sampaloc chapel.

**Do you have Wi-Fi?**
Yes, in the rooms, reception and the function hall; weak at the kubos and
the pool. The password is given at check-in.

**Is there a kiddie pool?**
Yes, 2 feet deep, open 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM. The main pool is 4 to 6 feet
deep, open until 10:00 PM, with night swimming until the lights go off.

**Is there a lifeguard?**
No. Children swim with an adult; life vests are free at reception, and a
first-aid kit is kept there.

**How do we get there by public transport?**
Bus or UV Express to Tanay from Cubao or Shaw, then a Sampaloc or Daraitan
jeep from the Tanay market to the chapel, then a three-minute walk. The
last jeep back leaves at around 6:00 PM.

**Can we reschedule?**
Once, free, with at least seven days' notice, to a date within 90 days.
Less notice forfeits the reservation fee.

**What if it rains on our date?**
The resort stays open in ordinary rain and closes only for a typhoon
signal or a road closure, in which case the booking is rescheduled free.
No refund is given for rain.

**Can we hold a birthday party or an event?**
Yes: birthdays, reunions, outings and small weddings, up to 40 guests, or
60 with exclusive use arranged with the owners. Reserving the function
hall costs ₱3,000; decorations with painter's tape only.

**Can we reserve the whole resort?**
Exclusive use is arranged directly with the owners at 0918-224-7710, not
on the booking page.

**Can I talk to a person?**
Yes: Viber or SMS at 0918-224-7710 from 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Payments,
refunds, complaints and special requests are always handled by the family.
