# Alteza Hillside Resort — Rooms, Cottages and Capacity

Alteza has six accommodations, booked by the room. Every room has its own
bathroom with a hot shower, air-conditioning, and a small veranda. Each
room's rate for a date is shown by the booking page and by this chat's
availability check, which also state how many guests the rate includes
and the fee for each guest above that. The documents do not list prices.

Casa Uno is on the reception level, with no steps from the parking area.
It has one queen bed. Its rate includes two guests and it holds at most
three, with a folding bed added for the third.

Casa Dos is beside Casa Uno, one level down by the garden. It has one queen
bed and one single bed. The rate includes two guests and it holds at most
three.

Familia is the largest room, on the pool level. It has two queen beds and
one double-deck bunk. The rate includes four guests and it holds at most
six. It is the only room that can take a family of five or six in one
room.

Kubo A and Kubo B are the two native-style cottages at the far end of the
garden, with bamboo walls, a nipa roof and a concrete floor. Each has one
queen bed and a pull-out mattress. The rate of each includes two guests and
each holds at most four. The kubos are the quietest rooms and the farthest
from the function hall.

The Loft Suite is above the reception, reached by a wooden stair, with the
best view of the valley. It has one queen bed. The rate includes two
guests and it holds at most two; no extra guest can be added, because of
the stair and the size of the room.

Every room comes with pillows, blankets and bed linen, one bath towel per
included guest, and basic toiletries (soap and shampoo sachets). Extra
towels are available at reception for a small fee. Guests should bring
their own toothbrush, toothpaste and swimwear.

A guest above the number the rate includes, up to the room's maximum, is
charged the per-head extra-guest fee for the stay. The fee is shown with
every price. Children aged five and below are not counted and are not
charged; children aged six and above count as guests. Extra beds are not
available beyond the folding bed and pull-out mattresses described above;
an extra set of foam mattresses can be rented for the Familia room only.

Photos of every room are on the booking page.
