# Villa Amihan Private Resort — Overview

Villa Amihan is a privately owned, exclusive-use resort in Pansol, Calamba,
Laguna. "Exclusive use" means only one group occupies the property per booking
slot — there are no walk-in guests and the resort is never shared between two
parties on the same day.

The property address is 27 Sampaguita Lane, Barangay Bagong Kalsada, Pansol,
Calamba, Laguna. The gate is unmarked and painted dark green; guests are told to
look for the mango tree beside the gate rather than a signboard, because the
street numbering in Sampaguita Lane is not continuous.

Villa Amihan sits on a 620-square-metre lot. The house and covered areas take up
roughly 240 square metres, the pool deck another 180, and the rest is garden,
parking and the service yard at the back.

The house was built in 2016 as a family rest house. It was opened for private
rentals in March 2019 after the second bedroom wing and the kiddie pool were
added. It is still family-owned and managed directly by the owners — there is no
booking agency, franchise or resort chain involved.

Villa Amihan is a hot spring resort. The pool water is drawn from a private deep
well tapping the Mount Makiling geothermal aquifer, and comes out of the ground
between 38°C and 40°C. The water is not heated by a boiler or a heat pump; it is
naturally hot at the source, which is why there is no surcharge for "heated
pool" the way there is at resorts that heat their water.

The standard capacity is 30 guests. The absolute maximum the property is allowed
to hold, including children and drivers, is 45 guests. This cap comes from the
septic system's rated load, not from floor space, so it is not negotiable.

Two live-in caretakers, Mang Rudy and Ate Baby, stay in the staff quarters at the
back of the lot. They handle the gate, the pool draining and refilling, and the
turnover cleaning. They do not cook for guests, serve food, or wash guests'
dishes as part of the base rate.
