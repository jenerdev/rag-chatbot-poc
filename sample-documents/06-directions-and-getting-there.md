# Villa Amihan — Directions and Getting There

Villa Amihan is at 27 Sampaguita Lane, Barangay Bagong Kalsada, Pansol, Calamba,
Laguna. The pin sent in the confirmation message is accurate; typing the address
into a map application is not, because Sampaguita Lane is registered under its old
name, Purok 4 Extension, in several map databases.

## By car from Metro Manila

From Makati or Taguig, take SLEX southbound and exit at Calamba. Travel time is
about 90 minutes on a weekday morning and can reach three hours on a Saturday
morning during summer.

From the Calamba exit, turn left toward Pansol along the national highway and
drive about 4.5 kilometres. Sampaguita Lane is on the right, about 300 metres past
the Petron station at the corner of Bucal.

Sampaguita Lane is narrow and one vehicle wide for the last 80 metres. Buses and
coasters cannot enter. Groups arriving by coaster are dropped at the mouth of the
lane and walk the last 80 metres.

The gate is dark green and unmarked, with a large mango tree on its left. There is
no signboard because the barangay does not permit commercial signage on
residential lots.

## By public transport

From Buendia or Cubao, take a JAM Liner or DLTB bus bound for Santa Cruz, Laguna
and ask to be dropped at Pansol. The fare is around ₱150 and the ride is about two
hours.

From the Pansol drop-off point on the highway, a tricycle to Sampaguita Lane costs
₱50 to ₱70 for two passengers and takes five minutes. Tricycle drivers know the
lane by the old name, Purok 4 Extension, and by the mango tree.

## Nearby places

The nearest large supermarket is SM City Calamba, about 20 minutes away by car.
Most groups do their grocery run there before heading to the resort, since the
Pansol sari-sari stores carry only basics.

The nearest hospital is Calamba Medical Center, about 25 minutes away. The
barangay health centre is a five-minute tricycle ride and handles minor cases.

Enchanted Kingdom in Santa Rosa is about 35 minutes away by car. Nuvali is about
40 minutes. The Rizal Shrine in Calamba town proper is about 20 minutes.

## Arrival

The caretakers open the gate at the start of the booked slot and not before, since
the pool refill runs up to that time. Groups that arrive early wait in their
vehicles or at the covered area outside the gate.

Guests message 0917-845-2290 when they are about 15 minutes away so the gate is
open on arrival and the group is not blocking the lane.
