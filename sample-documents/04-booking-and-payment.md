# Villa Amihan — Booking, Payment and Cancellation

## How to book

Bookings are made on Villa Amihan's booking page. To open it, press the Book
button at the top of this chat. Choose the slot, the date and, for an Overnight
stay, the number of nights, then the number of guests. The page shows the exact
price for that date, including weekend and holiday rates, and sends a booking
request to the owners with your name, mobile number and email.

A booking request is not yet a booking. The owners reply, usually within the
same day, to confirm the date and to arrange the reservation fee. Questions can
still go to 0917-845-2290 on Viber or SMS, or to
villaamihan.bookings@gmail.com.

This chat can check whether a date is open, but it cannot make, change or
confirm a booking. Only the booking page and the owners can.

A date is only held once the reservation fee is paid. Villa Amihan does not hold
dates on a verbal or messaged promise, because doing so during the 2019 peak
season cost the owners three weekends.

## Reservation fee and balance

The reservation fee is ₱3,000 for the Day Tour and Overnight slots, and ₱5,000
for the 22-Hour Whole Day slot. The reservation fee is non-refundable but is
deducted from the total.

The reservation fee is paid through GCash to 0917-845-2290 under the account name
"R. Amihan Ventures", or by bank transfer to BPI savings account 3049-2211-87
under the same name. The sender must send a screenshot of the transfer; the
booking is confirmed by reply, not by the transfer alone.

The balance is paid in cash on arrival, before the group settles in. Villa Amihan
does not accept cheques, credit cards, or bank transfers for the balance, and
does not have a card terminal on site.

A refundable security deposit of ₱2,000 is collected in cash together with the
balance. It is returned at checkout after the caretakers check the property, less
the cost of any breakage or missing item.

## Cancellation and rescheduling

A booking may be rescheduled once, free of charge, if the request is made at
least seven days before the booked date. The new date must fall within 60 days of
the original, and if it falls on a higher rate tier the difference is paid.

A reschedule requested less than seven days before the booked date forfeits the
reservation fee. A new booking then requires a new reservation fee.

Cancellations do not get the reservation fee back at any notice period. This is
stated at the time of booking and is the single most common source of
disagreement, so it is repeated in the confirmation message.

If Villa Amihan cancels — a well pump failure, a typhoon, or a barangay-wide
water interruption — the reservation fee is refunded in full within three banking
days, or the booking is moved to any open date at the guest's choice.

The three peak dates of 24, 25 and 31 December cannot be rescheduled at all. A
change on those dates is treated as a cancellation.

Bookings for 24, 25 and 31 December open on 1 September each year and are taken
first-come, first-served on payment of the reservation fee.
