# Villa Amihan — House Rules

## Noise

Videoke, loud music and amplified sound must stop at 10:00 PM. This is Barangay
Bagong Kalsada Ordinance 2018-04, not a house preference, and the barangay tanod
does respond to complaints from the two adjoining houses.

After 10:00 PM guests may still swim, talk and play music at conversation volume.
The restriction is on amplified sound, not on being awake.

## Guest count

The number of guests declared at booking is what the caretakers expect at the
gate. Guests above the declared number are admitted and charged ₱250 per head in
cash at the gate; they are not turned away.

The hard cap of 45 people includes children of every age and any drivers who stay
on the property. Groups that arrive over 45 are asked to have the excess wait
outside, which has happened twice and is unpleasant for everyone.

## Food and drink

Guests may bring in any food and drinks, including alcohol, with no corkage fee.
Villa Amihan makes no money on food and has no interest in policing it.

A corkage fee of ₱1,500 applies only when an outside caterer sets up and serves on
the property, because the caterer uses the kitchen, the tables and the water, and
leaves behind the most cleaning.

Lechon deliveries are allowed and are common. The lechon must be set on the
concrete prep counter in the grilling area and not on the dining tables, because
the oil has ruined one table already.

## Pool rules

No glass containers of any kind on the pool deck. Drinks on the deck must be in
plastic, tin or tumblers. A broken glass means draining the pool, and the cost of
the refill is charged to the security deposit.

Guests must shower at the outdoor shower before entering either pool. Sunblock,
oils and lotion cloud the water and shorten the time between drainings.

Diving and jumping into the main pool are not allowed. At 4.5 feet, the deep end
is not deep enough for it.

Swimming diapers are required for children not yet toilet-trained. Regular
disposable diapers disintegrate and are the most frequent cause of an emergency
drain.

## Pets

Pets are welcome. Small and medium dogs and cats are allowed at ₱500 per pet,
with a limit of three pets per booking.

Pets are not allowed in either pool, on the beds, or inside the four bedrooms.
Owners clean up after their pets; the caretakers will not.

## Smoking and fire

Smoking is allowed outdoors only — the pool deck, the grilling area and the
garden. It is not allowed in the bedrooms or the function area, and the smoke
alarms in the hallway are live.

Fireworks, sky lanterns and bonfires are not allowed anywhere on the property at
any time of year, including New Year's Eve. Sparklers held by an adult are the
one exception.

## Checkout

Guests bag their own trash and leave it in the bin area at the back. Guests do not
have to sweep, mop, or wash dishes for the turnover, but leaving the dishes in the
sink unwashed is what the rule expects — dishes left on the tables and around the
pool are what draws a deduction.

Checkout times are firm: 5:00 PM for the Day Tour, 12:00 noon for the Overnight,
and 6:00 AM for the 22-Hour Whole Day. The pool refill for the next group starts
immediately after.
